# 旁白草稿（不调用TTS）
0–3 s: At this replication fork, the two DNA templates run in opposite directions.
3–8 s: Primase makes short RNA primers. Their exposed three-prime ends give DNA synthesis a starting point.
8–16 s: DNA polymerase adds new DNA only from five-prime to three-prime.
16–24 s: The leading strand grows continuously. The lagging strand starts repeatedly, making short Okazaki fragments.
24–30 s: RNA primers are processed away. Polymerase fills the DNA gaps, then ligase seals the remaining nicks.
